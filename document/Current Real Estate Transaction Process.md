## Current Real Estate Transaction Process in the Government  

### **Step 1**: Deposit (**Optional**)  
The buyer and seller sign a deposit contract and agree on a transaction date. A third party (anyone) witnesses the agreement.  

### **Step 2**: Notarization of the Transfer Contract  
The parties meet at a notary office to sign and notarize the **"Land Use Rights Transfer Contract."** Payment and land title documents are exchanged at this stage.  

### **Step 3**: Tax Declaration at the Tax Office  
The parties submit all necessary documents to the tax office to pay taxes:  
- **Seller**: Pays **2%** personal income tax.  
- **Buyer**: Pays **0.5%** registration tax.  

### **Step 4**: Title Transfer  
The buyer submits all documents to the land registry office to complete the ownership transfer process.  



## Applying This Process to the Current Website  

### **Case 1: Using VND (Traditional Process)**  
After the buyer selects a property, they contact the seller and complete all the transaction steps **offline**. 
The website only serves as a record-keeping tool for reference and lookup.  

Once all procedures are completed, the government updates the ownership status in the system.  

### **Case 2: Using Ethereum (Fully Online Process)**  

#### **Step 1: Deposit**  
After selecting a property on the website, the buyer creates a deposit contract and sends it to the seller.  
- The website provides a contract template, and the buyer only needs to adjust details like the deposit percentage and the remaining payment timeline.  
- The seller may receive multiple offers but can only accept one contract for signing.  

#### **Step 2: Creating the Ownership Transfer Contract**  
- The buyer sends the **ownership transfer contract** to the seller.  
- If the seller agrees, they confirm the contract.  
- A notification is sent to the **notary** for approval.  
- Once the notary verifies and approves, **funds and ownership rights are automatically transferred**. Taxes are deducted and transferred to the government’s account.  

Alternatively, the **deposit step** can be skipped altogether.
